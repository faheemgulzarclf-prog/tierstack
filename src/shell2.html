FAQ

Questions traders ask

Will this make me money for sure?

No. It multiplies an edge you already have. If your numbers lose, the plan shows that honestly.

Which risk style should I pick?

Low if you are new or on a losing run. Medium follows the guide. High passes faster but fails more tests. All three stay inside the firm's loss limits.

Why do my two tests take turns?

If both trade the same week, one bad week can fail both. Taking turns means a bad week only hits one.

Which prop firms does it work with?

Any firm with a two-step test. Pick your firm on the Firm rules page and its numbers fill your plan.
